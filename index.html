Each cartographer gets a bottle of ink. When it runs dry — or when mischief is complete — you pass the pencil. The atlas remembers who drew every scribble.

margin note: all maps are lies we agree to walk on.

Three chapters, then blood.

Hot-seat. Pass the machine. Do not “help” during someone else’s turn unless they ask. Helping is how forests appear inside lava.

CHAPTER I

Draw the land

Stamp grass, rivers, ice sheets, pits and magma. Paper itself is walkable. Water slows you. Lava bites. Pits are rude.

CHAPTER II

Build the nonsense

Walls, locked doors, bridges over water, bounce pads, conveyor belts, campfire checkpoints, paired portals, signs with your handwriting.

CHAPTER III

Stock the bestiary

Drop slimes, bats, charging knights, mimic chests. Sprinkle coins, keys, hearts. Then someone has to walk through it.
